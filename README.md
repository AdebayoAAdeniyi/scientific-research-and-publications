## Research approach and contribution

My work connects mechanistic chemical modeling with biological and materials questions. Across collaborations, I have applied quantum chemistry, molecular modeling, structure-based drug design, molecular dynamics, spectroscopy, computational electrochemistry, and immunoinformatics. More recently, I expanded into machine learning and chemical language models for natural product generation. This portfolio helps readers find **specific evidence through DOI-linked publications**, rather than relying on a general list of skills.

## Recent MSc research — AI for natural product discovery (2026)

**Thesis:** *Source-specific conditional CLMs for natural product discovery: biosynthetic and ionization-aware deep learning* — MSc Computing Science, University of Alberta (2026).

Research focus: source-specific RNN and transformer chemical language models for generating natural-product candidates; conditional generation guided by molecular scaffolds and properties, chemical representation, and evaluation of molecule novelty, validity, and source specificity. Work is distinguished from the peer-reviewed paper list above. **Thesis research and models are not represented as published articles; associated code/data should be linked when authorized and publicly released.**

Additional recent investigations include computational exploration of TIM-3/galectin-9 and Cav1.2/IRE1α molecular interactions, with research status described in [projects](docs/theses-and-projects.md).

## Bibliography provenance and quality notes

The 71 records and DOI strings were **transcribed and lightly normalized from a supplied publication list**, not individually validated against Crossref or publisher databases. Titles and journal abbreviations may differ from the publisher versions. **Publication #3 has no DOI in the supplied list.** The CSV identifies each record by its original bibliography number. The seven subject collections classify each paper once for browsing; some research is intrinsically cross-disciplinary. Please consult DOI landing pages, [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-2421-1382) and [Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=QDAoQt8AAAAJ) for authoritative records and newer publications.

## Scholar metric updates (optional)

Google Scholar does **not** provide a standard public, free author-metrics API for dependable automatic GitHub updates. This repository includes an **optional weekly GitHub Actions workflow using SerpApi**, which may require an account and paid API usage. It is **not active** until you provide the `SERPAPI_KEY` repository secret and enable GitHub Actions with write permission. Failed API calls leave the last valid snapshot unchanged; the live profile link always remains available. [Setup instructions](docs/metrics-setup.md).
